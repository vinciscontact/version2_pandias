# Hotel Pandia's: standard operating procedures (SOPs)

For every branch. Each role has a daily routine and a few rules that are never broken.
These match the staff app (Today, Orders, Tables, Menu, Bills, Customers, Feedback, Team, Reports, Close the day).
Draft for the founder to review and adjust before printing.

---

## 1. Founder / owner

**Every day (10 minutes, any time)**
1. Open **Today** with "All branches". Check each branch's sales, open tables and alerts.
2. Look at low ratings (1–3 stars) across branches. Each should be marked resolved by its manager the same day.
3. Check **discounts and voids** for anything unusual (large amounts, the same person every day).

**Every week (Monday)**
1. **Reports → Branch comparison, Last week.** Note the weakest branch and talk to its manager.
2. **Dish sales:** dishes that never sold go on the list to rethink. Dishes the kitchen often "can't make" mean a stock problem.
3. **Activity log:** skim price changes, unpaid closes and voids.

**Every month (by the 5th)**
1. Open the **Monthly report** (made automatically on the 1st). Download it as PDF for the records, and as Excel for the accountant.
2. Review the GST summary and payment reconciliation with the accountant.
3. Approve menu price changes, if any, and **Publish to website**.
4. Review staff accounts: deactivate anyone who has left.

**Every quarter**
1. **Quarterly report** for each branch: compare with the previous quarter and with the same quarter last year.
2. Decide menu changes, branch targets and staffing.

**Data (12-month rule)**
- When the archive banner appears (30 days before), **download the archive first**, store it safely (two places), then tap **Archive and remove**. Never ignore the banner. Nothing is removed until you confirm.

**Never**
- Share your owner login. Every manager and staff member gets their own account.
- Empty **Recently removed** without reading what it will delete.

## 2. Branch manager

**Opening (before the first guest)**
1. Sign in. Check the date and branch at the top are right.
2. **Menu → Sold out today:** switch off anything not available today (ask the kitchen). Everything else should be on.
3. Write today's note on the **notice board** (specials, "mutton runs out at 9", short staff).
4. Check every kitchen tablet and the desk screen show **Live** (not "Reconnecting").
5. Check the QR cards are on every table, clean and not damaged.

**During service**
1. Keep **Today → Needs you now** empty:
   - **Confirm guests (Seated ✓)** within 2 minutes of a new table ordering. Check people are really sitting there.
   - **Late food (20+ min):** go to the kitchen, then to the table and reassure the guest.
   - **Low rating:** go to the table or call the guest the same day; mark resolved with what you did.
2. **Discounts** only with a real reason, chosen from the list. Every discount is reported.
3. **Removing a dish from a bill** needs your manager PIN and a reason. Don't share your PIN.

**Closing**
1. **Close the day:** settle every open table: paid, walk-out (with a note) or void (with a reason).
2. **Cash count:** count the drawer and enter the amount. A difference over ₹100 needs a note.
3. Read the **day-end summary**. Anything unusual goes in the note for the owner.

**Every week**
- **Feedback:** every low rating resolved.
- **Team:** shifts and attendance (if used) are correct.
- **Stock and purchases** (if used): purchases entered, low stock reordered.

**Never**
- Close a table as "paid" without the money received.
- Let staff use your account or your PIN.

## 3. Cashier / desk

**Opening**
1. Sign in with **your own** account. Check the branch and date.
2. Check the receipt printer has paper, and the UPI QR stand is on the counter.

**During service**
1. Watch **Tables:**
   - **Gold, "Confirm guests":** check the table, set the right number of guests, tap **Seated ✓**. Their order goes to the kitchen only then.
   - **Green, "Wants bill":** print the bill and take it to the table or call them to the counter.
2. **Taking payment:**
   - Choose **Cash**, **UPI** or **Card** exactly as the guest paid.
   - Split payments: enter each part separately (e.g. UPI ₹600, then Cash ₹240).
   - For UPI, **check the money has arrived** on the shop's phone or soundbox before tapping UPI.
3. After full payment the table shows **Paid**. Tap **Close table** once the guests leave, so the next party can order.
4. A guest lost their phone or can't see their bill: tell them the **table PIN** (on the table's bill screen) to open it on another phone.
5. **Waiter calls:** send someone, then tap **Done**.

**Closing**
1. No table should be left open. Ask the manager about any that can't be settled.
2. Count cash with the manager for the cash count.

**Never**
- Mark a bill paid before the money is received.
- Close an unpaid table yourself. Call the manager.
- Give discounts. Only the manager can.

## 4. Waiter / captain

**Opening**
1. Sign in on your phone (captain mode). Check your **section** (your tables).
2. Check your tables: clean, QR card in place, water and plates ready.

**During service**
1. Greet the guests and point to the **QR card**. For guests who won't scan (elderly, no phone), **take the order on your phone** in captain mode, with the right guest count.
2. When a guest's phone shows **"Waiting for a server to confirm your table"**, tell the desk, or tap **Seated ✓** yourself if your role allows.
3. **Ready to serve** on the kitchen board means take it to the table now. Read the **guest name** on the ticket so each plate reaches the right person.
4. **Allergy or "no onion"** notes are in red. Double-check with the kitchen before serving.
5. **Wrong dish or complaint:** apologise, tell the manager, and ask for a **Remake** (no extra charge). Never just remove the dish yourself.
6. Answer **waiter calls** within 2 minutes.
7. At the end of the meal, remind guests to rate their meal on the screen; the Google review button appears after it.

**Never**
- Take cash for the bill at the table without bringing it straight to the desk.
- Change a guest's order without telling them.

## 5. Kitchen

**Opening**
1. Turn on the kitchen tablet, open **Orders**, tap **Sound on**, and check it says **Live**.
2. Tell the manager what is **not available today**, so it's switched off before guests order.

**During service**
1. New ticket (chime) → tap **Start cooking** when you begin. → **Ready** when the plate is at the pass. The waiter taps **Served**.
2. Cook in the **order the tickets arrived**, oldest first. Amber means it's getting long; red means it's late, so tell the manager.
3. **Read the whole ticket:** spice level (mild / medium / spicy), **parcel** (pack it to take away), and notes. **Red notes are allergies.** Follow them exactly.
4. A dish **can't be made** (ran out, burnt): tap **Can't make** on that dish. It comes off the guest's bill and their phone tells them. Then tell the manager.
5. Something runs out completely: switch it off in **Menu → Sold out today**. It disappears from every table at once.

**Closing**
1. Every ticket should be served or cleared. Nothing left in New or Cooking.
2. Enter **today's usage and wastage** (if stock tracking is used).

**Never**
- Leave a ticket in "New" after you've started it. Guests watch the status on their phones.
- Ignore a red allergy note.

## 6. When something goes wrong

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Guest leaves without paying** (walk-out) | Manager closes the table as **Walk-out** with a note (table, amount, what happened). Never mark it paid. |
| **Order looks like a prank** (table empty) | Don't tap Seated ✓. The order stays waiting and never reaches the kitchen. Tell the manager. |
| **Guest complaint** | Waiter apologises → manager comes to the table → **Remake** (no charge) or **remove the dish** with the manager PIN and reason. Mark the feedback resolved. |
| **Dish ran out after ordering** | Kitchen taps **Can't make**. The waiter offers an alternative. |
| **Kitchen screen offline** | The desk sees a warning. Print the open tickets from the desk and carry them to the kitchen. Fix Wi-Fi / restart the tablet. |
| **Internet down at the branch** | Switch the router to the 4G backup. If still down, take orders on paper, enter them in the app once it's back, and tell the manager. |
| **Guest's phone died / new phone** | Desk gives the **table PIN**. The guest opens the QR on any phone and enters it. |
| **Wrong payment method entered** | Don't try to fix it yourself. The manager voids the payment with a reason and records it again. |
| **Cash doesn't match at closing** | Recount with the manager. Enter the counted amount and explain any difference in the note. |
| **Staff member leaves the job** | The manager or owner deactivates their account the same day. |
| **Archive banner appears (12 months)** | Owner downloads the archive first, then confirms. Nobody else can. |
