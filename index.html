Our biryani

Our biryani is golden, not fiery red. The spice stays in the background so every grain tastes of slow-cooked meat. Food writers have called it subtle and a little more golden than most. We take that as a compliment.

Ask a North Madras regular what to order and you'll hear the same three things: kuska, the biryani rice without the pieces; a soft, flaky parotta; and a bowl of mutton gravy to bring them together.
